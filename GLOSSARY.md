# Glossary: DRAM Capacitor Hole Etch

Terms are defined as they are used in this book. Chapter references point to the main discussion.

---

## A

**ACL (amorphous carbon layer):** PECVD carbon hard mask for the hole etch. Reference 1.40 µm with a 40 nm SiON cap; ≈ 1350 nm after the mask open. (Ch. 2.2)

**Acceptance angle:** Largest ion angle that reaches a given depth without striking the wall; arctan(w/h). About 0.7° edge to edge for the finished hole including the mask. (Ch. 3.2.3)

**Actinometry:** Ratio of an emission line to a nearby Ar line to track relative species density (e.g., F 704 / Ar 750). (App. B.5)

**APC (advanced process control):** Feed-forward from mask CD and mold thickness and feedback from bottom CD and bow, plus consumable-age offsets. (Ch. 15.4)

**ARDE (aspect-ratio-dependent etching):** Fall of the bottom etch rate as the hole deepens. ER/ER₀ = 1/(1 + kA). (Ch. 3.4)

**ARDE coefficient (k):** Parameter of the linear ARDE form. Reference 0.020 (fluorocarbon, 20 °C); ≈ 0.012 (cryogenic HF). (Ch. 3.4, 14.1)

**Array efficiency:** Fraction of the die area occupied by the cell array; 0.55 in the reference product. Sets global loading. (Ch. 3.6)

---

## B

**BO (bottom-open) step:** Final step that clears the bottom SiN stop and lands on W. Reference CHF₃/O₂/Ar, 30 s, reduced bias. (Ch. 7.2, 12.3)

**Bottom CD:** Hole diameter at the pad. Reference 24 nm; ≥ 20 nm required on every hole. (Ch. 1.4.3, 12.2)

**Bottom stop:** 20 nm SiN etch-stop layer at the base of the mold, above the landing pads. (Ch. 2.1.4)

**Bow:** Widening of the hole below the neck, from reflected, wide-angle, and deflected ions. Reference 33.5 nm at 350 nm depth. (Ch. 10)

**BPSG:** Boron-phosphorus-doped silicate glass; the reference lower oxide. Etches ≈ 17% faster than TEOS in the plasma and several times faster in HF. (Ch. 2.1.2)

**Bridging:** Merger of two neighbouring holes (and later their pillars) through a broken wall. Hole-level limit 10⁻⁹ per pair. (Ch. 10.4)

---

## C

**CCP (capacitively coupled plasma):** Parallel-plate reactor used for the capacitor hole: 40 MHz source, 400 kHz bias, 30 mm gap. (Ch. 5)

**CD families:** Groups of holes with different mean CD created by multiple patterning (core/gap sides of SADP). (Ch. 2.3.2)

**CD-SAXS:** Transmission small-angle X-ray scattering that measures the average hole profile and tilt. (Ch. 15.2)

**Clausing factor (K):** Transmission probability of a tube; K ≈ 4d/(3L) for long tubes. 0.023 at full depth. (Ch. 3.3.2)

**Coburn–Winters model:** Γ_bottom/Γ_top = K/(K + β(1 − K)). (Ch. 3.3.3)

**CO knee:** Bend in the CO (483 nm) emission during ME2 that marks the median arrival of holes at the bottom stop. (Ch. 15.1.3)

**COB (capacitor over bit line):** DRAM architecture in which the capacitor sits above the bit line. (Ch. 1.2.1)

**Cryogenic etch:** Hole etch with the wafer at −40 to −70 °C using HF-containing chemistry. (Ch. 4.6, 8.4, 14.1)

**C_s (storage capacitance):** Cell capacitance. Reference 8.9 fF (9.7 fF full sidewall). (Ch. 1.3)

**Cylinder capacitor:** Thin TiN shell lining the hole, used inside and out; replaced by the pillar below ≈ 30 nm CD. (Ch. 14.4)

---

## D

**DC superposition:** Negative DC voltage on the upper electrode that sends ballistic electrons to the wafer and relieves hole charging. (Ch. 6.4)

**Double SADP:** Two self-aligned double-patterning line sets at 60° that define the honeycomb holes. (Ch. 2.3.1)

---

## E

**Effective selectivity:** Mold removed divided by mask lost over the whole etch; ≈ 2.6 in the reference, against a blanket selectivity of 5.5. (Ch. 2.2.3)

**Electron shading:** Isotropic electrons cannot reach the bottom of a deep hole, so the upper wall charges negative and the bottom positive. (Ch. 3.5)

**EOT (equivalent oxide thickness):** Thickness of SiO₂ with the same capacitance per area as the dielectric stack; 0.50 nm reference. (Ch. 1.3.2)

**ESC (electrostatic chuck):** Ceramic chuck that clamps and cools the wafer; four heater zones and two He zones in the reference. (Ch. 8.3)

---

## F

**Facet:** Sloped, rounded top corner of the mask opening; grows to ≈ 20° by the end of the etch. (Ch. 13.2)

**Focus ring:** Si or SiC ring around the wafer that sets the edge sheath; its erosion tilts edge holes. (Ch. 8.5, 9.2)

---

## G

**G(h):** ARDE-corrected depth h + k h²/(2w); step times are differences of G divided by the layer rate. (Ch. 7.2.2, App. E.2)

**Gouge:** Depth of W removed from the landing pad by the BO step; ≤ 10 nm. (Ch. 12.3)

---

## H

**Honeycomb:** Hexagonal lattice of capacitor holes, 45 nm pitch in the reference. (Ch. 1.2)

**HV-SEM (high-voltage SEM):** SEM at 10–30 kV that images the hole bottom through the mold; main tool for bottom CD and placement. (Ch. 11.6, 15.2)

---

## I

**IED (ion energy distribution):** Distribution of ion energies at the wafer; broad and bimodal (0.3–6 keV) under 400 kHz bias. (Ch. 3.2, 6.1)

---

## L

**Landing pad (LP):** W pad, 26 nm wide, that moves the contact point from the bit-line grid to the honeycomb. (Ch. 1.4)

---

## M

**ME1, ME2:** Main etch steps through the upper (TEOS) and lower (BPSG) oxides. (Ch. 7.2)

**Middle support:** 50 nm SiN layer at 770–820 nm depth that holds the pillars and resets the hole's polymer and charge. (Ch. 2.1.3, 11.2.3)

**Mold:** The 1.6 µm stack of oxides and nitride supports through which the hole is etched and which is later removed. (Ch. 2.1)

---

## N

**Neck:** Narrowing near the top of the hole where sticky polymer collects. Reference 30 nm at 80 nm depth. (Ch. 10.2)

**Not-open:** Hole that fails to reach the landing pad. Limit 1×10⁻⁸ per hole (≈ 170 per die). (Ch. 12)

---

## O

**OCD (optical critical-dimension metrology):** Model-based scatterometry of the average hole profile. (Ch. 15.2)

**Overetch:** Time in ME2 after the median hole reaches the stop; 29 s in the reference. (Ch. 7.2.2)

---

## P

**Pillar capacitor:** Solid TiN pillar formed in the hole; dielectric and plate wrap its outside after mold removal. (Ch. 1.3.1)

**Π (polymerization index):** (C − O_feed − O_wafer)/F for the gas mix. Working range 0.30–0.45. (Ch. 4.2)

**Placement budget:** Allowed bottom-to-pad offset, |Δ| ≤ 9 nm, with overlay, pad, twisting, and tilting contributors. (Ch. 1.4.2, 11.5)

**Pulsing (bias):** On/off modulation of the bias (reference 2 kHz, 40% duty in late ME2) to relieve charging. (Ch. 6.2)

---

## R

**Residence time (τ):** pV/Q; ≈ 6.6 ms in the reference chamber. (Ch. 5.4)

**Ring lift:** Actuator that raises the focus ring as it erodes to keep edge tilt near zero. (Ch. 8.5.2)

---

## S

**Seasoning:** Running dummy wafers after PM until the walls reach a steady state. (Ch. 9.3.3)

**Side punch:** Etch into the pad-isolation SiN beside an offset pad during BO; ≤ 30 nm. (Ch. 12.3.3)

**SN1, SN2:** Nitride steps through the top and middle supports. (Ch. 7.2)

**Specular reflection:** Mirror-like reflection of grazing keV ions from the wall; lets ions reach the bottom and makes the bow. (Ch. 3.2.3, 10.3)

**Striation:** Vertical grooves on the hole wall from mask-edge roughness; ≤ 2.0 nm (3σ). (Ch. 13.3)

**Support (top, middle):** SiN layers in the mold that hold the pillars after the oxide is removed. (Ch. 2.1.3)

---

## T

**Tailored waveform:** Non-sinusoidal bias that holds the sheath near a set voltage, narrowing the IED. (Ch. 6.3)

**Taper:** Narrowing of the hole with depth; 0.22° in the reference lower oxide. (Ch. 12.1.2)

**Tilting:** Systematic lean of holes in a region, mainly outward at the wafer edge. (Ch. 11.4)

**Top support:** 120 nm SiN at the top of the mold; forms the neck. (Ch. 2.1.3)

**Twisting:** Random hole-to-hole bending of the hole below about A = 28; 3σ ≤ 5 nm at the bottom. (Ch. 11.2–11.3)

---

## V

**VC (voltage contrast) inspection:** E-beam inspection that shows open holes (grounded through the pad) brighter than not-open holes. (Ch. 12.5)

---

## W

**WAC (waferless autoclean):** O₂/NF₃ plasma after every wafer that removes the previous wafer's wall polymer; 60 s. (Ch. 9.3.2)

**Wall (between holes):** Oxide between neighbouring holes, p − CD; 13 nm at the top, ≈ 11.5 nm at the bow. (Ch. 1.2.2, 10.4)
